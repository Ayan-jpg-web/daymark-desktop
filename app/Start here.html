DAYMARK / OFFLINE GUIDE

Make room for what matters.

Your gallery and clock are already on the desktop. Try Show beside System stats. Both stay visible. Drag either heading to find a home for it.

Your first five minutes

  1. Find the gallery: launch Daymark Desktop again, or right-click one of its widgets and choose Open widget gallery.
  2. Personalize: choose a gallery accent, switch the clock between 12h and 24h, or show Sticky note and choose Edit note. Type your name or a reminder in Notepad, then press Ctrl+S. Keep the existing text encoding.
  3. Arrange: drag the heading. Right-click → Settings → Draggable to lock its location. Hide removes a widget from view, not from your library. Your note and positions are local.
  4. Install more: Gallery → Install .rmskin → Choose .rmskin file. For a practice skin, open Examples/Daymark-Today.rmskin inside the app folder. Inspect the contents, leave Load included skins enabled and click Install.
  5. Manage: choose Manage skins to see imported packages and load or unload individual widgets. Each folder is a separate widget; two variants in the same folder replace one another. Save layout lets you name and restore arrangements. The Daymark starter layout is a safe way back.

Weather is your choice

Set up only opens an offline city picker. Choosing a city, or confirming Other location, contacts Open-Meteo. Use coordinates for a nearby city rather than a home address. The provider receives the coordinates and sees your IP. Live weather refreshes on load and about every 30 minutes while open. Disconnect clears the saved endpoint and stops future requests. Hiding stops polling but keeps its setup. Data is credited to Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). See provider terms if redistributing or using it commercially.

Privacy and troubleshooting

No account, online gallery, analytics, location detection or automatic update check. Independent third-party Rainmeter skins are not sandboxed: they can run programs or make network requests. Only install trusted skins; review any requested plugins or layouts. Software updates are manual.

If another copy of Rainmeter is running, existing widgets are not moved into Daymark. Exit that other copy before installing a .rmskin: the upstream skin installer restarts its host. For Daymark, use the gallery importer instead of the system-wide file association.

Setup installs for your user under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Daymark Desktop. Notes and other edits are under Skins\Daymark\@Resources; saved layouts are in Layouts. Back up these folders and Rainmeter.ini before reinstalling or moving computers. The portable edition should live in a writable folder. Do not run it from inside the ZIP. To quit, right-click the Rainmeter tray icon and choose Exit.
